The square of an even whole number is even.
The square of an odd whole number is odd.
Every whole number is even or odd.
No whole number is both even and odd.
A whole number is even exactly when it is twice a whole number.
The square of a whole number is a whole number.
The square of twice a whole number is four times the square of that whole number.
If four times a whole number equals twice another whole number, then that other whole number is twice the first.
Whenever a ratio of two whole numbers equals the square root of two, the square of the first equals twice the square of the second.
Two even whole numbers share two as a common divisor.
A fraction in lowest terms is a ratio of two whole numbers that share no common divisor greater than one.
Every ratio of two whole numbers equals some fraction in lowest terms.
