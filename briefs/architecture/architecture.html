Supporting claims

Each compound edge is checked through these atomic claims. The first two support judged_by (and, with the two comes_from edges, judges); the last three support combines.

  • the `check` module asks an `Oracle` whether a witness entails a sentence
  • the `check` module asks an `Oracle` whether supporting sentences establish a claim's sentence
  • `Support::Sentence` wraps a `Box<Proposition>`
  • `Proposition` has `And`, `Or`, and `Not` variants, whose operands are other propositions
  • `check` computes the value of `and`, `or`, and `not` from the values of their operands, without asking the oracle
