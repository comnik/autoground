# Ground a formal specification in the codebase

> [!NOTE]
> Work in progress.

Working with a codebase often involves creating and maintaining derived artifacts, most
commonly documentation for different audiences or formal specifications. Even if the
artifact is correct at the time of creation, it can quickly drift and become inconsistent as
the codebase evolves.

What is derived and what is ground truth depends on the context. Often, the codebase is
considered ground truth. But in some cases, the codebase itself may be considered derived,
e.g. from requirements found on Slack or in meeting transcripts.
